The People Who Raised Me 🤍

Home isn't perfect, but it's still home.
My favorite people ♡

One thing you'll probably notice about my family is our faces. We've always been misunderstood because of our resting facial expressions. People often think we're angry or in a bad mood, when in reality we're simply... existing.

It's funny how something so small can create so many first impressions. But once people get to know us, they'll realize we're loud, playful, and full of laughter.

Every birthday, Christmas, and family gathering becomes my favorite memory. A table full of food, everyone talking at once, endless teasing, and laughter that somehow gets louder every year those moments remind me what home truly feels like.

“Love isn't always loud. Sometimes it's simply showing up for each other.”
