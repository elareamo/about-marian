The path I'm taking

Ever since I was little, I dreamed of becoming a pilot. I loved the thought of flying above the clouds, traveling the world, and discovering new places while doing what I loved.

As I grew older, I began pursuing that dream and even reached the CAAP process. Along the way, I learned how important it was to have a college degree as a foundation. That realization led me to choose Geodetic Engineering.

If I'm honest, there were moments when I questioned my decision and wondered if I had taken the wrong path. Choosing FEATI wasn't the future I originally imagined, but it became the place where I met incredible friends and discovered a different version of myself.

I don't know exactly where life will take me yet, but I do know this: I'll keep chasing a future that makes my younger self proud.

“Not every journey follows the map we planned, and that's okay.”
