The Future I'm Chasing ✈️

Sometimes dreams don't disappear. They simply change direction.
The dream I had
The future I'm building

Ever since I was little, my biggest dream was to become a pilot. I loved the idea of flying above the clouds, exploring new places, and turning every destination into part of my journey.

As I grew older, I pursued that dream and even reached the CAAP process. Along the way, I realized how important it was to have a college degree as a foundation. That decision eventually led me to choose Geodetic Engineering.

Although it wasn't the future I originally imagined, I slowly discovered a different kind of adventure. Surveying, mapping, drones, GNSS technology, and creating accurate maps showed me that there are many ways to help shape the world around us.

I still carry the little girl who dreamed of airplanes, but today I'm also becoming someone who dreams of building communities through geospatial engineering. Whatever path life chooses for me, I hope to make my younger self proud.

“Not every journey follows the map we planned, and that's okay.”
